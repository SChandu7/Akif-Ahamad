A cardiologist who also builds

Dr. Akif trained in Vijayawada, Mumbai and Guntur, and now works as a consultant cardiologist in Guntur. His daily work is in the cath lab and the clinic: angiograms, angioplasties, pacemakers and echocardiography.

Outside the clinic, he teaches. His lectures help DM, DrNB, MD and MBBS students learn cardiology for the ward, not just the exam. That teaching grew into a set of platforms for learning, research, blood donation and hospital care.
